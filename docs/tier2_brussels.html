Tier-2 Hydra and Anansi System Update

Start: 2026-11-05 00:00:00
End: 2026-11-10 00:00:00
Updated: 2026-11-04 12:03:07
Type: planned
Impact level: low

We are updating the operating system on VUB Tier-2 clusters Hydra and Anansi with the latest security and bug fixes. Access to these clusters will be unavailable for 15-30 min on Thursday 5th November at 02:00 CET. We will reboot the login nodes and the OnDemand service of portal.hpc.vub.be, disconnecting any active user sessions on those systems.

The roll-out of updates to the computes nodes will start on Thursday Nov 5th and will be progressively carried out in the coming days. This operation has a minimal impact on our users: compute capacity will be slightly lower during the update, but there will be no downtime of the clusters and all job queues will remain operational.

Planned maintenance

Tier-2 Hydra Zen4 nodes unavailable for network maintenance

Start: 2026-11-17 13:00:00
End: 2026-11-17 17:00:00
Updated: 2026-11-06 18:59:16
Type: planned
Impact level: medium

A hardware maintenance operation will be carried out on the main network switch connecting the Zen4 nodes of Hydra on November 17th 13:00 CET. This operation is expected to last for a few hours.

During the intervention no jobs will be allowed to run on the *zen4* partition of Hydra. Users will be able to submit jobs to the *zen4* without interruptions, but those jobs will stay in queue until the maintenance ends.
