The data center hosting Tier-1 **sofia** will carry out maintenance tests on 2,
3 and 4 December. All **sofia** compute nodes will be shut down during this
period. The OnDemand web portal, login nodes and Globus remain accessible.

A reservation will be put in place to ensure no jobs are running when the
shutdown starts.  Queued jobs that cannot finish before the maintenance window
will remain in the queue and will automatically start as soon as the cluster is
back online and resources become available.

More info will be provided later.
