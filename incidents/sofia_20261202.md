title: Tier-1 sofia - planned data center maintenance
start_date: 2026-12-02 06:00:00

level: high
planned: yes
---

The data center hosting Tier-1 **sofia** will carry out maintenance tests on 2,
3 and 4 December. This operation requires the shutdown of all compute nodes of
**sofia**. Specifically partitions `zen5_dense`, `zen5_himem` and `zen4_h200`.

We will also use this opportunity to carry out system updates on **sofia** and
other maintenance tasks that cannot easily be done with the cluster online. A
reservation is in place to ensure that no jobs will be running at the time of
shutdown. Queued jobs will stay in the queue and will be automatically
launched as soon as the cluster is back online.

The cluster and its storage will become accessible with restrictions on Dec 2nd
at 12:00 CET. At this time the following services will be operational:

* OnDemand web portal on visualization and login nodes
* SSH terminal interface on login nodes
* Globus service

The remaining compute nodes will be available at the end of the maintenance.
